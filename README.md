# Love

Love is a multilingual Bible reader for focused reading, study, search, and personal reflection. It brings Bible versions, commentaries, notes, bookmarks, highlights, and reading preferences together in one app.

No account setup is required, and the included reading content is available offline after installation.

## Download Love

[Download the latest version](https://github.com/ParkSnoopy/Love/releases/latest)

Choose the file for your device:

- **Android:** `.apk`
- **Windows:** `.zip`
- **macOS:** `.zip`
- **Linux:** `.tar.gz`

After downloading an archive, extract it and open Love. On Android, open the downloaded APK and follow your device's installation prompts.

## Included content

Love includes multiple Bible versions in:

- Korean
- English
- Simplified Chinese
- Japanese

English and Korean Bible commentaries are also available. Open **Settings** to choose the Bible version and optional commentary you want to use.

## Start reading

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Choose your **App Language**.
3. Select an **Active Bible**.
4. Optionally select an **Active Commentary**.
5. Open **Reader** from the bottom navigation bar.

Your reading position is remembered automatically.

## Read and navigate

In **Reader**:

- Tap the book and chapter name near the bottom to open the book and chapter picker.
- Use the left and right chapter buttons to move between chapters.
- Tap the Bible name at the top to switch versions.
- Tap a verse to select it.
- Long-press a verse to begin selecting multiple verses.

When one or more verses are selected, you can:

- Copy the selected text
- Add or remove bookmarks
- Add or remove highlights
- Write or edit a note
- Open related commentary

Saved notes can also appear directly beneath their corresponding verses. Use the memo button at the top of Reader to show or hide them.

## Read commentaries

Choose a commentary in **Settings** to make commentary available while reading. The commentary pane follows the current book and chapter and can be resized or opened full screen.

When background information is available, use the information button to read the introduction for the current book.

## Search the Bible

Open **Search** to find verse text or jump directly to a book and chapter.

Example searches:

- `Genesis 26`
- `창세기 26장`
- `삼상 17`
- A word or phrase from a verse

Search can be limited to:

- The whole Bible
- The Old Testament
- The New Testament
- Commentary
- The active Bible version
- Bible versions or commentaries in a selected language

Tap a result to return to that passage in Reader.

## Review saved study items

Open **Saved** to view three organized sections:

- **Bookmarks**
- **Highlights**
- **Notes**

Tap a saved item to return to its passage. Notes can be reopened for editing, and saved items can be removed when no longer needed.

## Personalize the reading experience

Open **Settings** to adjust:

- App language
- Serif or sans-serif reader font
- Named font styles from the lightest to the darkest available face
- Font size
- Line spacing
- Interface scale

The **Custom** theme lets you choose a Light or Dark base and a primary color.

## Back up and restore app data

Under **Settings → App Data**:

- **Backup app data** exports your settings, reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes, and history.
- **Import app data** restores a Love backup file.

Importing replaces the current settings and saved study data. Confirm that you selected the intended backup before continuing.

## Report a problem

Open **Settings → Support → Check update** to compare your installed version with the latest release. When an update is available, Love provides a link to its download page.

To report a problem, open **Settings → Support → Report a bug**. Love opens a report page and copies recent error details for you to review before submitting.
